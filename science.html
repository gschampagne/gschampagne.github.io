Science

I am a Physics student at McGill University passionate about experimental design, imaging, and detector physics. My research explores how we can use technology and creativity to make invisible processes measurable — from neutrino detection to metabolic imaging.

In 2025, I was a DAAD RISE Germany Scholar at the University of Kiel, where I worked on deuterium metabolic imaging using a 7T MRI system. Previously, I conducted experimental nuclear physics research in the Brunner Neutrino Lab, contributing to the nEXO collaboration and building custom electronics, DAQ systems, and simulations.

Selected Projects

bSSFP Simulation (University of Kiel)

Simulated balanced steady-state free precession MRI sequences for deuterated metabolites and analyzed 7T data in MATLAB and Paravision.

Parahydrogen Bubbler

Designed and tested a parahydrogen generation system, automating bubble flow and optimizing magnetic resonance signal strength.

Gadolinium Relaxivity Study

Prepared and analyzed gadolinium samples to determine T₁ and T₂ dependence on concentration at 1T and 7T magnetic fields.

Pressure Sensor Box (Brunner Neutrino Lab)

Developed a Raspberry Pi–based gas monitoring system with live GUI visualization and PCB integration for lab safety applications.

nEXO Scale Model

3D printed and programmed a 1:100 scale model of the proposed nEXO neutrino detector, integrating LEDs and Python-based event simulation.

Stepper Motor Control Software

Developed modular Python code for high-precision actuator control with stepper motors and rotary encoders, enhancing hardware automation.

Chroma Stave Scanner

Simulated photon transport to characterize silicon photomultipliers (SiPMs) in cryogenic detector staves using Chroma and Matplotlib.

Compton Scattering Experiment

Measured photon scattering spectra to verify Compton’s formula and determine the electron mass using Klein–Nishina analysis.
